Privacy Policy
Last updated: 23.11.2026

Overview
Lithuanian Language A1-A2 respects your privacy. The app does not require an account, has no backend servers, and does not collect, store, or share your personal data.

Analytics and crash reports
If you choose to share analytics and diagnostics data with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous, aggregated information, such as app usage statistics and crash reports. This data cannot be used to identify you personally. You can turn this off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

Subscriptions and payments
Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment details or Apple ID. For more information, see Apple's Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Data stored on your device
Your learning progress and settings are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, this data is removed.

Children's privacy
The app does not knowingly collect any data from children.

Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.

Contact
If you have any questions, contact us at: lithuanianlanguageapp@gmail.com
